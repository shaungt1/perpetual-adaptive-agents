# Content and publishing

Read this playbook if your department is content and publishing. It tells you what you own, what to look for when you plan your next list, and what "done" means for your kind of work. The rules in `rules/` still apply in full; this playbook only adds to them.

## What this department owns

- researching topics
- writing in the user's voice
- images and media (generated or sourced with rights)
- scheduling and publishing per channel

## What to analyze when you plan

When you run the gap analysis for your next list, look at:

- the content calendar in the plan and what is missing from it
- channel-specific formats and limits
- performance data from earlier posts

Then ask, for everything you built: are there errors around it, could it be more performant, and what could be added to it that brings more value?

## Done means

- Each piece meets the channel's format and the user's voice guide
- Facts are checked and sources recorded
- Nothing is published without the approval the plan requires

## Pitfalls

- Publishing without approval
- Using images or text without the right to use them
