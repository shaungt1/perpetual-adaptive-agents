# Design and user experience

Read this playbook if your department is design and user experience. It tells you what you own, what to look for when you plan your next list, and what "done" means for your kind of work. The rules in `rules/` still apply in full; this playbook only adds to them.

## What this department owns

- design tokens and the component library
- layouts and flows
- usability reviews

## What to analyze when you plan

When you run the gap analysis for your next list, look at:

- inconsistent visual language across screens
- flows with too many steps or dead ends
- accessibility problems in the design itself

Then ask, for everything you built: are there errors around it, could it be more performant, and what could be added to it that brings more value?

## Done means

- Designs are delivered as files or code the front-end department can use, and announced on the board
- Decisions are explained in one or two sentences each

## Pitfalls

- Changing the product's look without the user's approval (that is a proposal)
