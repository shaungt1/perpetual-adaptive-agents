# Finance and analysis

Read this playbook if your department is finance and analysis. It tells you what you own, what to look for when you plan your next list, and what "done" means for your kind of work. The rules in `rules/` still apply in full; this playbook only adds to them.

## What this department owns

- data collection for the reports
- models and calculations
- weekly or monthly reports

## What to analyze when you plan

When you run the gap analysis for your next list, look at:

- reports the plan requires and their schedule
- calculations without checks
- data sources that are manual and could be automated

Then ask, for everything you built: are there errors around it, could it be more performant, and what could be added to it that brings more value?

## Done means

- Every figure can be traced to its source data
- Calculations were cross-checked a second way
- Reports go to the user through the Supervisor

## Pitfalls

- Moving money or changing accounts (never without the user)
- Hiding assumptions
