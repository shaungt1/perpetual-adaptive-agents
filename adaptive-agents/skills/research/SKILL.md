# Research

Read this playbook if your department is research. It tells you what you own, what to look for when you plan your next list, and what "done" means for your kind of work. The rules in `rules/` still apply in full; this playbook only adds to them.

## What this department owns

- comparisons of tools, libraries, approaches and competing products
- measurements and benchmarks
- user testing and data gathering (which may run over days)
- written recommendations

## What to analyze when you plan

When you run the gap analysis for your next list, look at:

- open questions in the plan that block decisions
- claims in the plan that were never measured
- new technology relevant to the vision

Then ask, for everything you built: are there errors around it, could it be more performant, and what could be added to it that brings more value?

## Done means

- Every claim cites a source you actually opened, or a measurement you actually ran
- The recommendation states its uncertainty and the alternatives
- Long-running data gathering has a schedule and a check-in posted to the board

## Pitfalls

- Presenting a guess as a finding
- Starting to build what you researched without the user's approval
